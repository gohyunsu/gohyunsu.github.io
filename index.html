01 / Research direction

Perception, action, feedback.

I am interested in 3D visual understanding for grasping and object rearrangement: when to act, when to observe again, and how to adapt from the outcome.

01

Perceive

Recover task-relevant 3D state from visual evidence.

02

Decide

Choose a grasp, another view, or a revised plan.

03

Verify

Compare expected and observed outcomes before continuing.

Long-term direction Language-guided, multi-step manipulation.
